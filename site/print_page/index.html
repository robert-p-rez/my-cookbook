    Cheesy Thumbprint Appetizers with Hot Pepper Jelly

    Savory cheddar thumbprint bites filled with sweet and spicy hot pepper jelly.

    Ingredients

    • 1 cup all-purpose flour
    • 1/2 cup unsalted butter, softened
    • 1 cup shredded sharp cheddar cheese
    • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder
    • 1/4 teaspoon salt
    • 1/4 teaspoon black pepper
    • 1 tablespoon Dijon mustard (optional)
    • 1/4 cup hot pepper jelly (or more to taste)

    Instructions

    1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or lightly grease it.

    2. Make the dough:
      In a large bowl, combine softened butter, flour, shredded cheddar cheese, garlic powder, salt, and pepper. Add Dijon mustard if using. Mix until a smooth, cohesive dough forms.

    3. Shape:
      Roll dough into 1-inch balls and place on prepared baking sheet about 1 inch apart.

    4. Create indentations:
      Use your thumb or the back of a spoon to press a small well into the center of each ball.

    5. Bake:
      Bake for 12–15 minutes, until edges are golden brown.

    6. Add jelly:
      Let cool for about 5 minutes. Spoon approximately 1/2 teaspoon hot pepper jelly into each indentation.

    7. Serve:
      Allow jelly to set slightly. Serve warm or at room temperature.

    Prep Time: 10 minutes
    Cook Time: 15 minutes
    Total Time: 25 minutes
    Servings: 24 appetizers

    Classic Deviled Eggs

    Creamy, tangy deviled eggs perfect for gatherings and holidays.

    Ingredients

    • 12 large eggs
    • 1/3 cup mayonnaise (or half mayo and half plain Greek yogurt)
    • 2 tablespoons pickle relish (or finely diced dill pickle)
    • 1 1/2 teaspoons Dijon mustard (or yellow mustard)
    • Salt and freshly ground black pepper, to taste
    • Paprika, for garnish

    Instructions

    1. Place eggs in a saucepan and cover with cold water. Bring to a boil.
    2. Once boiling, cover, remove from heat, and let sit for 10–12 minutes.
    3. Transfer eggs to an ice bath and cool completely.
    4. Peel eggs and slice in half lengthwise. Remove yolks and place them in a bowl.
    5. Mash yolks with mayonnaise, relish, mustard, salt, and pepper until smooth.
    6. Spoon or pipe filling back into egg whites.
    7. Sprinkle lightly with paprika before serving.

    Store covered in the refrigerator until ready to serve.

    Greek Beet Salad with Yogurt

    Source: Maria Koutsogiannis
    Date: 2023-10-18

    Ingredients

    • 4 large red beets
    • 1 cup Greek yogurt
    • 2 tbsp olive oil
    • 3 garlic cloves, minced
    • Juice of half a lemon
    • 1/2 tsp salt
    • 1/3 cup chopped walnuts
    • 2 tbsp fresh chopped mint
    • 2 tbsp fresh chopped dill

    Instructions

    1. Roast the Beets

    1. Wash the beets thoroughly and place them in foil.
    2. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
    3. Place the foil-wrapped beets on a baking sheet.
    4. Roast for 40–50 minutes, or until fork tender.
    5. Unwrap the beets and allow them to cool completely.
    6. Peel the skins off using your fingers or a vegetable peeler.

    2. Slice the Beets

    Slice the peeled beets into wedges, about 6 wedges per beet, and set aside.

    3. Prepare the Yogurt Base

    In a small bowl, combine:

    • Greek yogurt
    • Olive oil
    • Minced garlic
    • Lemon juice
    • Salt

    Mix until smooth.

    4. Assemble the Salad

    1. Spread the yogurt mixture onto a low bowl or serving platter.
    2. Arrange the beet wedges on top of the yogurt.
    3. Sprinkle with walnuts.
    4. Add chopped mint and dill.
    5. Finish with a pinch of salt if desired.

    Serving

    Serve immediately.

    Cream Cheese Sausage Balls

    A rich, tender, cheesy sausage ball recipe using cream cheese for extra moisture and flavor.

    Ingredients

    For the Sausage Balls

    • 1 lb spicy pork sausage, ground and at room temperature
    • 1 (8 oz) block cream cheese, softened
    • 2 cups sharp cheddar cheese, shredded
      or pepper jack for heat
    • 1 1/4 cup Bisquick
    • 2 stalks scallions, finely chopped (optional)
    • 1/2 teaspoon garlic powder (optional)

    For the Dipping Sauce

    • 2 tablespoons mayo
    • 2 tablespoons sweet chili sauce
    • OR use honey mustard (as referenced in the original recipe)

    Instructions

    1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C). Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or lightly grease it.

    2. In a large mixing bowl, combine the softened cream cheese and sausage.
      Use your hands or a mixer to blend until evenly mixed.

    3. Add shredded cheddar, Bisquick, scallions (if using), and garlic powder.
      Mix until everything is fully incorporated and a uniform dough forms.

    4. Roll the mixture into 1–1.5 inch balls and place them on the prepared baking sheet.

    5. Bake for 20–25 minutes, or until the sausage balls are golden brown and cooked through.

    6. While baking, prepare the dipping sauce by mixing mayo and sweet chili sauce until smooth.

    7. Serve warm with dipping sauce on the side.

    Honey Cream Cornbread

    Ingredients

    • 1 cup all-purpose flour
    • 1 cup yellow cornmeal
    • 1/4 cup white sugar
    • 1 tablespoon baking powder
    • 1 cup heavy cream
    • 2 large eggs, lightly beaten
    • 1/4 cup vegetable oil
    • 1/4 cup honey

    Instructions

    1. Preheat the oven to 400°F (200°C).
      Lightly grease a 9x9-inch baking pan.

    2. Mix dry ingredients:
      In a large bowl, stir together the flour, cornmeal, sugar, and baking powder.
      Form a well in the center.

    3. Add wet ingredients:
      Add the heavy cream, eggs, vegetable oil, and honey.
      Stir until well combined.

    4. Bake:
      Pour the batter into the prepared baking pan.
      Bake for 20–25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.

    5. Cool and serve:
      Let cool slightly before slicing.
      Serve warm, optionally topped with butter and additional honey.

    Apple Cinnamon French Toast Poppables

    Freezer-friendly breakfast bites and a smart way to use up leftover bread (including crusts).

    Ingredients

    Custard Base

    • 8 eggs
    • 2 cups milk
    • 3/4 cup brown sugar
    • 1 tbsp cinnamon
    • Splash of vanilla
    • Dried bread cubes (enough to soak up the custard)
    • 2 cups peeled and finely chopped apples

    Crumb Topping

    • 3/4 cup brown sugar
    • 3/4 cup flour
    • 1 tsp cinnamon
    • 2–3 tbsp melted butter or margarine (enough to make a “wet sand” texture)

    Instructions

    1. Cut leftover bread (or crusts) into cubes and let them dry out overnight.
    2. In a large bowl, whisk together eggs, milk, brown sugar, cinnamon, and vanilla.
    3. Fold in the dried bread cubes and chopped apples. Add just enough bread so the liquid is absorbed.
    4. Divide mixture into 24 greased muffin tins (or use silicone pans).
    5. Mix the crumb topping ingredients and sprinkle over the top.
    6. Bake at 350°F for 25 minutes.
    7. Cool completely before freezing. Reheat in the microwave or toaster oven.

    Apple Muffins with Crumb Topping & Maple Syrup Icing

    Ingredients

    Muffins

    • 1 and 3/4 cups all-purpose flour or whole wheat
      OR replace 3/4 cup with protein pancake mix
    • 1 teaspoon baking soda
    • 1 teaspoon baking powder
    • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
    • 1/2 teaspoon salt
    • 1/2 cup softened unsalted butter
      or replace 1/4 cup of the butter with applesauce for a healthier version
    • 1/2 cup packed light or dark brown sugar
    • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
    • 2 large eggs, at room temperature
    • 1/2 cup Greek yogurt (unsweetened or vanilla), at room temperature
    • 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
    • 1/4 cup milk (any kind), at room temperature
    • 1 and 1/2 cups peeled & chopped apples

    Crumb Topping

    • 1/4 cup dark brown sugar
    • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
    • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
    • 1/4 cup melted unsalted butter
    • 2/3 cup all-purpose flour or whole wheat flour

    Maple Syrup Icing

    • 1 cup confectioners’ sugar
    • 3 tablespoons half and half
    • 1 teaspoon maple syrup

    Instructions

    1. Preheat oven to 425°F. Spray a muffin pan with nonstick spray or line with cupcake liners.
      Recipe makes 12–14 muffins.

    2. In a large bowl, whisk together the flour, baking soda, baking powder, cinnamon, and salt. Set aside.

    3. In a separate bowl, beat the butter and both sugars on high speed until smooth and creamy (about 2 minutes).
      Add the eggs, yogurt, and vanilla. Beat on medium speed for 1 minute, then increase to high speed until the mixture is combined and mostly creamy.
      It’s ok if it looks slightly curdled. Scrape down the bowl as needed.

    4. With the mixer on low speed, add the dry ingredients and milk into the wet mixture. Mix until no flour pockets remain.
      Fold in the chopped apples.

    5. Make the crumb topping:
      In a medium bowl, mix the brown sugar, granulated sugar, and cinnamon. Stir in the melted butter, then gently mix in the flour with a fork.
      Keep the mixture as large crumbles and do not over-mix (overmixing turns it into a paste).

    6. Spoon the batter evenly into muffin cups, filling each to the top. Add crumb topping to each and gently press so it sticks.

    7. Bake for 5 minutes at 425°F, then (without opening the oven) reduce temperature to 350°F.
      Bake an additional 15–18 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
      Total bake time: 20–23 minutes.

    8. Allow muffins to cool in the pan for 5 minutes, then transfer to a wire rack.

    9. Make the icing:
      Whisk together all icing ingredients. Drizzle over warm or cooled muffins.

    10. Store iced or plain muffins at room temperature for 2–3 days, or in the refrigerator for up to 1 week.

    Blueberry Butter Swim Biscuits

    Soft, buttery buttermilk biscuits baked in a butter bath and topped with a sweet glaze.

    Ingredients (makes ~9 biscuits)

    Butter Bath

    • 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, melted

    Biscuit Dough

    • 2 1/2 cups all-purpose flour
    • 1/2 cup granulated sugar
    • 1 tablespoon baking powder (aluminum-free preferred)
    • 1/2 teaspoon salt
    • 1 3/4 cups buttermilk (add a splash more if dough seems dry)
    • 1 1/2 cups fresh or frozen blueberries

    Glaze (optional but recommended)

    • 1 cup powdered sugar
    • 2–3 tablespoons milk

    Instructions

    1. Preheat & prep:
      Preheat oven to 450°F (232°C). Lightly grease a 9x9-inch (or 8x8-inch) baking dish.

    2. Butter bath:
      Pour melted butter into the baking dish, swirling to coat the bottom evenly.

    3. Mix dry ingredients:
      In a medium bowl, whisk together flour, sugar, baking powder, and salt.

    4. Add buttermilk:
      Stir in buttermilk until a sticky dough forms. Add a splash more if needed.

    5. Fold in blueberries:
      Gently mix in blueberries to avoid bursting.

    6. Bake:
      Spoon the dough over the melted butter in the dish and spread gently.
      Lightly score into 9 squares with a knife.
      Bake on the middle rack for 25–30 minutes, until golden and a toothpick inserted comes out clean.

    7. Glaze & serve:
      While baking, whisk powdered sugar and milk to desired drizzling consistency.
      Drizzle over warm biscuits. Let cool slightly, slice, and serve.

    Snickerdoodle Bars

    Ingredients

    • 3/4 cup unsalted butter, fully melted
    • 3/4 cup packed brown sugar
    • 1 cup granulated sugar
    • 2 large eggs
    • 2 teaspoons pure vanilla extract
    • 2 cups all-purpose flour
    • 1 teaspoon baking powder
    • 1 teaspoon cream of tartar
    • 1/2 teaspoon salt

    Topping

    • 1/4 cup granulated sugar
    • 2 teaspoons cinnamon

    Instructions

    1. Preheat oven to 350°F (175°C).
    2. In a large bowl, combine melted butter, brown sugar, and granulated sugar. Mix well.
    3. Add eggs and vanilla extract. Mix until fully incorporated.
    4. In a separate bowl, whisk together flour, baking powder, cream of tartar, and salt.
    5. Add dry ingredients to wet ingredients and mix until combined.
    6. Line a 9x9-inch baking dish with parchment paper.
    7. Evenly spread batter into prepared baking dish.
    8. In a small bowl, mix together granulated sugar and cinnamon for the topping.
    9. Sprinkle topping evenly over the batter.
    10. Bake for 27 minutes, or until set and lightly golden.
    11. Allow to cool completely before slicing.

    Yield

    • Makes 8 large bars

    Sweet Potato Coffee Cake

    Moist, warmly spiced coffee cake made with mashed sweet potatoes and topped with a cinnamon crumb.

    Ingredients

    Cake Batter

    • 3/4 cup cane sugar
    • 1/2 cup melted butter
    • 2 eggs
    • 2 tablespoons maple syrup
    • 2 cups mashed sweet potatoes (about 2 large; boiled, peeled, and mashed)
    • 2 cups all-purpose flour
    • 2 teaspoons baking powder
    • 2 teaspoons pumpkin spice
    • 1/4 teaspoon salt

    Cinnamon Layer

    • 2 tablespoons coconut sugar or brown sugar
    • 1 teaspoon cinnamon

    Crumb Topping

    • 5 tablespoons flour
    • 1/4 cup coconut sugar or brown sugar
    • 1/4 cup cane sugar
    • 1/4 teaspoon cinnamon
    • 1/4 cup softened butter

    Instructions

    1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Line a 9x9-inch baking dish.

    2. In a large bowl, combine cane sugar, melted butter, eggs, maple syrup, and mashed sweet potatoes. Mix with a hand mixer for about 1 minute. (Some small sweet potato clumps are fine.)

    3. Add flour, baking powder, pumpkin spice, and salt. Stir until a thick batter forms.

    4. Pour half of the batter into the prepared pan.
      Sprinkle evenly with coconut or brown sugar and cinnamon.
      Pour the remaining batter over the top.

    5. In a small bowl, mix flour, sugars, cinnamon, and softened butter until crumbly. Sprinkle over the batter.

    6. Bake for 45 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

    7. Cool on a wire rack for about 20 minutes before slicing and serving.

    Carrot Cake Pancakes

    These carrot cake pancakes are rich, warmly spiced, and topped with a maple cream cheese drizzle. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

    Ingredients

    Pancakes

    • 2 cups finely grated carrots (about 6 large or 8 small)
    • 1 cup (125 g) whole wheat pastry flour (or whole wheat or all-purpose flour)
    • 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder
    • 1/2 teaspoon baking soda
    • 1/2 teaspoon fine salt
    • 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon
    • 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg
    • 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger
    • 1 large egg
    • 2 tablespoons coconut sugar or brown sugar
    • 1 cup buttermilk
    • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
    • Avocado oil or butter, for cooking

    Maple Cream Cheese Topping

    • 4 oz cream cheese, softened
    • 2 tablespoons maple syrup (plus more for serving)
    • 2 tablespoons milk (adjust for consistency)
    • 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
    • Dash of cinnamon
    • Optional: chopped toasted walnuts or pecans

    Instructions

    1. Prepare Ingredients

    1. Soften cream cheese by letting it sit at room temperature.
    2. Place grated carrots in a microwave-safe bowl, cover with a damp paper towel, and microwave for 2 minutes.
    3. Let carrots cool.
    4. Optional: preheat oven to 200°F to keep pancakes warm.

    2. Mix Dry Ingredients

    In a large bowl, whisk together:

    • flour
    • baking powder
    • baking soda
    • salt
    • cinnamon
    • nutmeg
    • ginger

    3. Mix Wet Ingredients

    In a separate bowl:

    1. Whisk egg and sugar.
    2. Add buttermilk and vanilla.
    3. Stir in cooled carrots.

    4. Combine Batter

    1. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients.
    2. Stir until just combined.
    3. Let batter rest for 5 minutes.

    5. Make Topping

    1. Whisk cream cheese until smooth.
    2. Add maple syrup, milk, vanilla, and cinnamon.
    3. Adjust sweetness and consistency as desired.

    6. Cook Pancakes

    1. Heat a skillet or griddle over medium-low heat (or 350°F).
    2. Lightly grease the surface.
    3. Scoop 1/4 cup batter per pancake.
    4. Cook 2–3 minutes until bubbles form and edges set.
    5. Flip and cook 1–2 minutes more until golden.
    6. Repeat with remaining batter.

    7. Serve

    • Top pancakes with maple cream cheese.
    • Add chopped nuts if desired.
    • Serve with additional maple syrup.

    Storage

    • Refrigerate leftovers up to 4 days
    • Freeze up to 6 months

    Easy Raspberry Jam (No Pectin)

    A simple 3-ingredient raspberry jam that is sweet, tangy, and beginner-friendly. This recipe uses no added pectin and can be stored in the fridge, freezer, or canned for long-term storage. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

    Equipment

    • 6 glass jars (8 oz / 250 ml)
    • Large heavy-bottom saucepan
    • Silicone spatula or wooden spoon
    • Potato masher or fork
    • Canning funnel
    • Food thermometer
    • Sieve or jelly bag (optional)
    • Bubble remover (optional)
    • Magnetic lid wand (optional)
    • Canning pot
    • Canning tongs

    Ingredients

    • 2.2 lb raspberries (about 8 cups)
    • 4 cups white sugar (28 oz)
    • 1/4 cup lemon juice (fresh or bottled)

    Instructions

    1. Prepare the Berries and Jars

    1. Sterilize jars, lids, and bands by washing in hot soapy water or running through a dishwasher.
    2. Allow jars to dry and keep them warm until ready to use.
    3. Place 1–2 small plates in the freezer for the gel test.
    4. If canning, prepare a water bath canner with a rack and enough water to cover jars by 1 inch.
    5. Wash raspberries thoroughly and remove stems or spoiled berries.

    2. Simmer the Jam

    1. Add raspberries and sugar to a large stainless-steel saucepan.
    2. Gently crush berries with a potato masher, leaving some whole if desired.
    3. Add lemon juice and stir.
    4. Bring mixture to a full rolling boil, stirring constantly so sugar dissolves.
    5. Skim foam from the surface as it forms.
    6. Simmer 15–20 minutes, until the jam thickens enough to coat the back of a spoon.

    3. Gel Test

    1. Remove pan from heat.
    2. Place 1 teaspoon jam on a frozen plate.
    3. Return plate to freezer for 1–2 minutes.
    4. Push jam with your finger — it should wrinkle if set.

    Alternative: Use a thermometer. Jam sets at 220°F (104°C).

    If not set, cook a few minutes longer and test again.

    4. Fill the Jars

    1. Place warm sterilized jars on a towel or wooden board.
    2. Ladle hot jam into jars leaving ¼ inch headspace.
    3. Tap jars gently or run a knife inside to remove air bubbles.
    4. Wipe jar rims clean.
    5. Add lids and tighten bands fingertip tight.

    At this point the jam can be:

    • used immediately
    • refrigerated for short-term storage
    • frozen for up to 6 months
    • water bath canned for long storage

    5. Water Bath Canning (Optional)

    1. Place filled jars in the canner so they are covered with at least 1 inch of water.
    2. Bring to a boil and process for 10 minutes (adjust for altitude).
    3. Turn off heat and let jars sit 5 minutes.
    4. Remove jars with tongs and cool on a towel 24 hours undisturbed.
    5. Check seals (lid center should not pop).

    Store sealed jars in a cool dark pantry for up to 12–18 months.

    Notes

    • Use ripe but not overripe raspberries for best flavor and natural pectin.
    • Always use non-reactive cookware (stainless steel, enamel, glass).
    • If jam is too runny, return it to the pan and cook longer.
    • For seedless jam, push finished jam through a fine mesh sieve.

    • Cinnamon or nutmeg
    • Fresh herbs (mint, thyme, basil)
    • Ginger
    • Chili peppers (jalapeño or habanero)
    • Liquor (amaretto, bourbon, schnapps)

    Ingredients

    • 1 cup celery, diced
    • 1 cup onions, diced
    • 1 cup cabbage, chopped
    • 2 cloves garlic, grated or pressed
    • 1 tablespoon butter (for sautéing vegetables)
    • 8 cups water or beef/chicken broth
    • 1 can diced tomatoes (28 oz or 1 quart home-canned)
    • 2 large beets or 3 medium beets
    • 1 large carrot or 2 medium, grated
    • 1 medium potato, diced
    • 1/2 cup fresh dill weed
    • Salt and black pepper, to taste
    • 1 bay leaf (optional)
    • Oil (for roasting beets)

    Instructions

    Roast the Beets

    1. Preheat oven to 375°F.
    2. Lightly coat beets with oil.
    3. Wrap beets in aluminum foil and place on a baking sheet.
    4. Roast for 45–60 minutes, until tender.
    5. Let cool for 10 minutes, then peel.
    6. Slice into 1/4-inch slices or dice as desired.

    Make the Soup

    1. In a large pot, sauté onions, celery, and cabbage in butter until soft and translucent.
    2. Add garlic and cook briefly.
    3. Add diced tomatoes and broth. Bring to a boil.
    4. Add roasted beets, grated carrots, diced potato, and bay leaf.
    5. Reduce heat and simmer until potatoes are tender, about 15–20 minutes.
    6. Stir in fresh dill, salt, and pepper.
    7. Remove bay leaf before serving.

    Storage

    • Cool completely before storing.
    • Freeze up to 3 months in airtight containers.
    • Thaw overnight and reheat gently on stovetop.

    Sweet 'n Sticky Baked Chicken Drumsticks

    Sweet, savory, and caramelized baked chicken drumsticks finished with a thick sticky glaze. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

    Ingredients

    • 1/2 cup balsamic vinegar
    • 1/2 cup honey
    • 1/2 cup packed light brown sugar
    • 1/4 cup low-sodium soy sauce
    • 3 garlic cloves, minced
    • 12 chicken drumsticks
    • 2 tablespoons sesame seeds
    • 1/4 cup chopped fresh flat-leaf parsley

    Instructions

    1. Marinate the Chicken

    1. In a large resealable bag, combine:
    2. balsamic vinegar
    3. honey
    4. brown sugar
    5. soy sauce

    6. minced garlic

    7. Add the chicken drumsticks and seal the bag.

    8. Marinate in the refrigerator for 2 hours.

    2. Bake the Chicken

    1. Preheat oven to 450°F (232°C).
    2. Line a rimmed baking sheet with aluminum foil.
    3. Place a cooling rack on top of the baking sheet.
    4. Remove chicken from the marinade, reserving the marinade.
    5. Arrange drumsticks on the rack.
    6. Bake for about 30 minutes, until skin is caramelized and dark in spots.

    3. Make the Sticky Glaze

    1. Pour the reserved marinade into a small saucepan.
    2. Bring to a boil, then reduce heat.
    3. Simmer about 15 minutes, until thickened.

    4. Finish the Chicken

    1. Brush the thickened glaze over the baked chicken.
    2. Return to the oven for 10 more minutes.

    5. Serve

    1. Transfer chicken to a serving platter.
    2. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and chopped parsley.
    3. Serve warm.

    Yield: 12 drumsticks

    Parmesan Crusted Crushed Turnips

    A delicious, crispy, low-carb alternative to crushed potatoes with a golden Parmesan crust. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

    Equipment

    • Large pot or stockpot
    • Potato masher
    • Pastry brush
    • Parchment paper
    • Rimmed baking sheet

    Ingredients

    • 12 small to medium turnips, peeled
    • Salt
    • 2 tablespoons olive oil
    • 3 cloves garlic, minced
    • Freshly ground black pepper, to taste
    • 1 cup freshly grated Parmesan cheese (or as needed)
    • Fresh chives, chopped (for garnish)

    Instructions

    1. Cook the Turnips

    1. Place peeled turnips in a pot and cover with salted water.
    2. Bring to a boil and cook for 20–30 minutes, until easily pierced with a knife.
    3. Drain and let cool slightly.

    2. Prep for Baking

    1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C).
    2. Place turnips on a clean kitchen towel or paper towels.
    3. Gently press each turnip down to about 1/2-inch thickness.
    4. Let drain for 15 minutes, then flip and allow the other side to dry.

    3. Season

    1. In a small bowl, combine:
    2. olive oil
    3. minced garlic
    4. salt
    5. black pepper

    4. Assemble

    1. Line a baking sheet with parchment paper or foil.
    2. Place flattened turnips on the sheet.
    3. Brush each with the oil and garlic mixture.
    4. Sprinkle Parmesan cheese over each, pressing lightly.
    5. Carefully flip each turnip and repeat seasoning and cheese on the other side.

    5. Bake

    1. Bake for 20–25 minutes.
    2. Flip each turnip.
    3. Bake an additional 15 minutes, until golden and crisp.

    6. Serve

    Garnish with chopped fresh chives and serve warm.

    Notes

    • Substitute Parmesan with Asiago, Romano, or Manchego if desired.
    • Flip quickly to keep the cheese coating intact.
    • Avoid overcooking to maintain texture.

    Instant Pot Option

    1. Add rack and 1 1/2 cups water (6-qt) or 2 cups (8-qt).
    2. Place peeled turnips on rack.
    3. Steam:
    4. Medium turnips: 5–6 minutes
    5. Small turnips: 3–5 minutes
    6. Use natural release.

    Storage & Make Ahead

    • Turnips can be boiled and crushed 1 day ahead.
    • Store on a towel in the refrigerator before baking.
