# Easy Homemade Chili

A simple and hearty chili that comes together quickly and pairs perfectly with cornbread or tortilla chips. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

## Ingredients

- 1 pound ground beef  
- 1 onion, chopped  
- 1 (15 oz) can tomato sauce  
- 1 (15 oz) can kidney beans  
- 1 (14.5 oz) can stewed tomatoes with juice  
- 1 1/2 cups water *(optional, for thinner chili)*  
- 1 teaspoon chili powder *(or more to taste)*  
- 1 pinch garlic powder  
- Salt and pepper to taste  

---

## Instructions

### 1. Brown the Meat
1. Place ground beef and chopped onion in a large saucepan over medium heat.  
2. Cook and stir until the beef is browned and the onion is tender, about **5–7 minutes**.

### 2. Add Remaining Ingredients
1. Stir in:
   - tomato sauce  
   - kidney beans  
   - stewed tomatoes with juice  
2. Add water if a thinner consistency is desired.

### 3. Season
Season the chili with:

- chili powder  
- garlic powder  
- salt  
- black pepper  

Bring the mixture to a boil.

### 4. Simmer
Reduce heat to low, cover, and simmer for **15 minutes**.

---

## Serving Suggestions

Serve with:

- Cornbread  
- Shredded cheese  
- Sour cream  
- Tortilla chips  
- Sliced jalapeños  
- Green onions
