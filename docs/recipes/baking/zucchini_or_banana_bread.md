# Zucchini or Banana Bread

A warmly spiced family quick bread that can be made with either zucchini or bananas.

## Ingredients

- 3 eggs
- 1 cup oil
- Choose one:
  - 2 cups unpeeled grated zucchini, packed
  - 2 ripe bananas, mashed
- 2 cups sugar
- 2 cups flour
- 1/4 teaspoon baking powder
- 2 teaspoons baking soda
- 1 teaspoon salt
- 3 teaspoons cinnamon
- 3 teaspoons vanilla
- 1 cup chopped nuts

## Instructions

1. Preheat the oven to **350°F (175°C)** and grease two loaf pans.
2. Mix the eggs, oil, and either the grated zucchini or mashed bananas.
3. Add the sugar, flour, baking powder, baking soda, salt, and cinnamon. Mix until combined.
4. Stir in the vanilla and chopped nuts.
5. Divide the batter evenly between the prepared loaf pans.
6. Bake for **45–60 minutes**, or until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

## Yield

Makes **2 loaves**.
