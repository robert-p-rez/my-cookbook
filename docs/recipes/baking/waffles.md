# Classic Waffles

These waffles turn out **light, crisp, and golden**. Perfect for breakfast any day of the week. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

## Ingredients

- 2 cups all-purpose flour  
- 1 teaspoon salt  
- 4 teaspoons baking powder  
- 2 tablespoons white sugar  
- 2 eggs  
- 1 1/2 cups warm milk  
- 1/3 cup butter, melted  
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract  

---

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare Ingredients
Gather all ingredients and preheat the waffle iron to your desired temperature.

### 2. Mix Dry Ingredients
In a large bowl, combine:

- flour  
- salt  
- baking powder  
- sugar  

Mix well and set aside.

### 3. Mix Wet Ingredients
In a separate bowl:

1. Beat the eggs.
2. Stir in the milk, melted butter, and vanilla extract.

### 4. Combine Batter
Pour the milk mixture into the flour mixture and beat until blended and smooth.

### 5. Cook Waffles
1. Ladle the batter into the preheated waffle iron.
2. Cook until waffles are **golden brown and crisp**.

### 6. Serve
Serve immediately with your favorite toppings.
