# Morning Glory Muffins

These cinnamon-spiced muffins are packed with whole wheat flour, apples, carrots, raisins, honey, and warm spices. They taste like a combination of apple cake, spice cake, and carrot cake—perfect for breakfast. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

## Ingredients

- 2 cups (260 g) whole wheat flour  
- 2 teaspoons baking soda  
- 2 teaspoons ground cinnamon  
- 1/2 teaspoon ground ginger  
- 1/2 teaspoon salt  
- 1/3 cup (35 g) ground flaxseed *(optional)*  

### Wet Ingredients

- 3 large eggs, room temperature  
- 1/2 cup (100 g) packed brown sugar  
- 1/4 cup (85 g) honey  
- 1/3 cup (80 ml) vegetable oil, canola oil, or melted coconut oil  
- 1/3 cup (80 g) unsweetened applesauce  
- 1 teaspoon orange zest *(optional)*  
- 1/3 cup (80 ml) orange juice or pineapple juice  
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract  

### Mix-ins

- 2 cups (260 g) shredded carrots (about 4 large)  
- 1 cup (140 g) shredded apple (about 1 large)  
- 1/2 cup (75 g) raisins  
- 1/2 cup (64 g) chopped pecans  

---

## Instructions

### 1. Preheat Oven
Preheat oven to **425°F (218°C)**.

Grease a **12-count muffin pan** or line with muffin liners.  
This recipe makes **14–16 muffins**, so prepare a second pan or bake in batches.

---

### 2. Mix Dry Ingredients
In a large bowl whisk together:

- whole wheat flour  
- baking soda  
- cinnamon  
- ginger  
- salt  
- flaxseed (optional)

---

### 3. Mix Wet Ingredients
In a separate bowl whisk together:

- eggs  
- brown sugar  
- honey  
- oil  
- applesauce  
- orange zest  
- orange juice  
- vanilla extract

Mix until fully combined.

---

### 4. Combine Batter
1. Stir carrots and shredded apple into the wet mixture.
2. Pour wet ingredients into the dry ingredients.
3. Stir a few times.
4. Add raisins and pecans.
5. Fold until just combined and no dry flour remains.

---

### 5. Fill Muffin Pan
Spoon batter into muffin liners, filling **all the way to the top**.

---

### 6. Bake
1. Bake **5 minutes at 425°F (218°C)**.
2. Without removing muffins, reduce oven temperature to **350°F (177°C)**.
3. Bake an additional **18 minutes**, until a toothpick inserted in the center comes out clean.

Total baking time: **23–24 minutes**.

---

### 7. Cool
Allow muffins to cool in the pan for **10 minutes**, then transfer to a wire rack.

---

## Storage

- Store covered at **room temperature for 2 days**
- Refrigerate for **up to 1 week**

### Freezing
Muffins freeze well for **up to 3 months**.  
Thaw overnight in the refrigerator and warm if desired.
