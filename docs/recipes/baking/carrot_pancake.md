# Carrot Cake Pancakes

These carrot cake pancakes are rich, warmly spiced, and topped with a maple cream cheese drizzle. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

## Ingredients

### Pancakes

- 2 cups finely grated carrots (about 6 large or 8 small)
- 1 cup (125 g) whole wheat pastry flour *(or whole wheat or all-purpose flour)*
- 1 1/2 teaspoons baking powder  
- 1/2 teaspoon baking soda  
- 1/2 teaspoon fine salt  
- 1 teaspoon ground cinnamon  
- 1/4 teaspoon ground nutmeg  
- 1/4 teaspoon ground ginger  
- 1 large egg  
- 2 tablespoons coconut sugar or brown sugar  
- 1 cup buttermilk  
- 1 teaspoon vanilla extract  
- Avocado oil or butter, for cooking  

---

### Maple Cream Cheese Topping

- 4 oz cream cheese, softened  
- 2 tablespoons maple syrup *(plus more for serving)*  
- 2 tablespoons milk *(adjust for consistency)*  
- 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract  
- Dash of cinnamon  
- Optional: chopped toasted walnuts or pecans  

---

## Instructions

### 1. Prepare Ingredients

1. Soften cream cheese by letting it sit at room temperature.  
2. Place grated carrots in a microwave-safe bowl, cover with a damp paper towel, and microwave for **2 minutes**.  
3. Let carrots cool.  
4. Optional: preheat oven to **200°F** to keep pancakes warm.

---

### 2. Mix Dry Ingredients

In a large bowl, whisk together:

- flour  
- baking powder  
- baking soda  
- salt  
- cinnamon  
- nutmeg  
- ginger  

---

### 3. Mix Wet Ingredients

In a separate bowl:

1. Whisk egg and sugar.  
2. Add buttermilk and vanilla.  
3. Stir in cooled carrots.

---

### 4. Combine Batter

1. Pour wet ingredients into dry ingredients.  
2. Stir until just combined.  
3. Let batter rest for **5 minutes**.

---

### 5. Make Topping

1. Whisk cream cheese until smooth.  
2. Add maple syrup, milk, vanilla, and cinnamon.  
3. Adjust sweetness and consistency as desired.

---

### 6. Cook Pancakes

1. Heat a skillet or griddle over **medium-low heat** (or 350°F).  
2. Lightly grease the surface.  
3. Scoop **1/4 cup batter** per pancake.  
4. Cook **2–3 minutes** until bubbles form and edges set.  
5. Flip and cook **1–2 minutes** more until golden.  
6. Repeat with remaining batter.

---

### 7. Serve

- Top pancakes with maple cream cheese.  
- Add chopped nuts if desired.  
- Serve with additional maple syrup.

---

## Storage

- Refrigerate leftovers up to **4 days**  
- Freeze up to **6 months**

---

## Notes

- To make buttermilk: mix 1 cup milk with 1 tbsp lemon juice or vinegar; let sit 5 minutes.  
- For dairy-free: use plant-based milk and dairy-free cream cheese.  
- Avoid overmixing batter to keep pancakes fluffy.
