# Purple Radish Salad

A quick and refreshing daikon radish salad with a tangy, slightly sweet sesame dressing. :contentReference[oaicite:0]{index=0}

---

## Ingredients

- 1 purple daikon radish, sliced into matchsticks  
- 1 carrot, sliced into matchsticks  
- 2 green onions, thinly sliced  

### Dressing

- 1 tablespoon toasted sesame oil  
- 1 tablespoon rice vinegar  
- 1 tablespoon honey *(or maple syrup for vegan)*  
- Juice of 1 lime  
- 1 clove garlic, pressed or minced  

### Garnish

- Toasted sesame seeds, to taste  

---

## Instructions

### 1. Prep Vegetables

1. Wash and trim the radish and carrot.  
2. Slice both into matchsticks using a knife or julienne peeler.  
3. Transfer to a mixing bowl and add sliced green onions.

---

### 2. Make Dressing

In a small bowl, whisk together:

- sesame oil  
- rice vinegar  
- honey (or maple syrup)  
- lime juice  
- garlic  

---

### 3. Assemble

1. Pour dressing over the vegetables.  
2. Toss until evenly coated.

---

### 4. Serve

- Serve immediately, or  
- Chill for a couple of hours for enhanced flavor  

Top with toasted sesame seeds before serving.

---

## Serving Suggestions

Serve alongside:

- Grilled meats  
- Veggie burgers  
- Rice or noodle dishes
